Research directions (from ASSESSMENT.md at commit 18c8a64, 7 Jul 2026):

- Rolling-horizon deployment: re-solve with observed states as condition-monitoring data arrive; the loop constraint becomes a terminal safety envelope.

- Scenario (sample average approximation) chance constraints: sample the empirical rainflow distribution and allow failure in at most floor(eps*S) scenarios; it measures what the Cantelli/Bernstein conservatism costs.

- Distributionally robust framing: Cantelli is the tight bound over the two-moment ambiguity set; this sets up a Wasserstein-ball extension for the sampling error of the moments.

- Decomposition for scale: components couple only through the assignment variables (Benders or Dantzig-Wolfe: an assignment master, per-component reliability subproblems).

- Policy baseline: compare against simple condition-based threshold policies.

- Big-M constants near the limits of epsilon (step 3 of the merge, item M31 of archive/merge-2026/step3/merged_review.md, 8 Nov 2026): the natural ranges used as big-M grow without bound as epsilon approaches 0.5 at Gaussian and Wiener cells and 1 at rainflow Cantelli and Hoeffding cells (1.6e7*tau^2 at epsilon = 0.4999), so a binary within Gurobi's integrality tolerance could switch a row off. Study tighter big-M constants (the largest value the reliability row allows on the cell) or an input limit on epsilon.

- To discuss (user, 8 Nov 2026): the numerical tolerance of the validators. The default of a check is derived from the Gurobi tolerances of the input (FeasibilityTol + IntFeasTol x M_s for a tracked quantity with natural range M_s; IntFeasTol for binaries; FeasibilityTol for a probability), because a binary that Gurobi accepts within IntFeasTol inside a big-M row can move a tracked state by IntFeasTol x M_s. Open: whether to polish the solution instead (fix the rounded binaries and re-solve the continuous part), which would allow the default FeasibilityTol; and whether chained rows can add up these gaps over many days (archive/merge-2026/step3/merged_review.md, item M19).
