Research directions (from ASSESSMENT.md, 7 Jul 2026):

- Rolling-horizon deployment: re-solve with observed states as condition-monitoring data arrive; the loop constraint becomes a terminal safety envelope.

- Scenario (sample average approximation) chance constraints: sample the empirical rainflow distribution and allow failure in at most floor(eps*S) scenarios; it measures what the Cantelli/Bernstein conservatism costs.

- Distributionally robust framing: Cantelli is the tight bound over the two-moment ambiguity set; this sets up a Wasserstein-ball extension for the sampling error of the moments.

- Decomposition for scale: components couple only through the assignment variables (Benders or Dantzig-Wolfe: an assignment master, per-component reliability subproblems).

- Policy baseline: compare against simple condition-based threshold policies.
